# Google Business Profile — Setup Checklist (FAB5 GeoSolutions)

A Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single biggest off-site lever for local
search. It powers the business panel on Google Search and Maps and reinforces
the structured data already embedded in the website. Set it up as a
**service-area business** so you don't have to publish a street address.

## Why it matters

Google cross-checks your website's structured data (already added in
`index.html`) against your GBP. When the **N**ame, **A**ddress/area, and
**P**hone ("NAP") match exactly, Google trusts the listing more and ranks it
higher. Keep the values below identical everywhere.

## Step-by-step

1. **Create the profile.** Go to https://business.google.com and sign in with
   the Google account you want to own the listing. Choose **Add your business
   to Google**.
2. **Business name:** enter `FAB5 GeoSolutions` exactly.
3. **Business type:** select **Service business** (not a storefront), so you can
   hide the physical address.
4. **Category:** set the primary category to **Engineering consultant** (or
   **Geotechnical engineer** if available). Add secondary categories such as
   *Mining consultant* and *Environmental consultant*.
5. **Service area:** add **Ontario, Canada**. You can add additional regions you
   serve. Skip entering a street address — service-area businesses keep it
   private.
6. **Contact info:** phone `+1 (705) 280-0432` and website
   `https://www.fab5geosolutions.com/`. Make sure these match the site exactly.
7. **Verify.** Google will offer verification by phone, email, video, or
   postcard. Complete it — the profile won't appear publicly until verified.
8. **Hours:** set Monday–Friday 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM (matches the website schema).
9. **Description:** paste a 1–2 sentence summary, e.g.:
   > FAB5 GeoSolutions provides geotechnical and mining engineering consulting —
   > tailings & dam safety, GISTM/TSM/CMSI compliance, governance, risk
   > assessment and technical advisory across the Americas, Oceania and the
   > South Pacific.
10. **Services:** add each service as a GBP service item, mirroring the website:
    Governance & Organizational Design · Conformance & Compliance Framework ·
    Technical Advisory · Innovation & Technology · Risk Assessment.
11. **Photos / logo:** upload the brand logo and, optionally, the social card at
    `/public/og-image.png`. Listings with images get more engagement.
12. **Languages:** note that the business serves clients in English and
    Portuguese.

## After it's live

- **Ask for reviews.** Send satisfied clients your GBP review link. Volume and
  recency of reviews are strong ranking signals.
- **Keep NAP consistent** anywhere the business is listed (LinkedIn, industry
  directories, association memberships). Inconsistent phone numbers or names
  dilute trust.
- **Post updates** (project milestones, certifications, events) every few weeks
  to keep the profile active.
- **Re-check hours** before holidays — Google lets you set special hours.

## Optional next steps for local SEO

- Add the business to relevant directories with identical NAP (e.g. mining and
  engineering association member listings).
- Consider a short list of client testimonials on the website to complement the
  GBP reviews.
